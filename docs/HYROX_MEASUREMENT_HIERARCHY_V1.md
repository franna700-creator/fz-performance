# FZ Performance — HYROX Measurement Hierarchy v1

Status: PREPARED ARCHITECTURE · item 28

## Purpose

The event-demand model answers **what HYROX requires**. The measurement hierarchy answers **which observations are most useful for deciding how prepared the athlete is, what remains under-measured, and what adaptation is most valuable next**.

The hierarchy is resolved from the active primary objective. HYROX is currently the primary objective in the prepared seed, but the measurement resolver is not hard-coded to Francois or to HYROX remaining primary forever.

## Primary evidence priorities

Highest-value evidence includes compromised-running repeatability, run fade, controlled running efficiency, station recovery cost, station work rate, sled capability and wall-ball tolerance. Secondary evidence includes transition cost, erg efficiency, carry/grip, repeated strength endurance and post-hybrid recovery cost.

This hierarchy intentionally includes **cost as well as output**. Completing a station quickly is not automatically positive if it creates disproportionate run fade, local tissue cost or loss of the next valuable training opportunity.

## Evidence quality

Preferred/direct event-relevant evidence outranks proxy evidence. Deadly Dozen may therefore provide useful proxy evidence for compromised running, carry/grip and repeated strength endurance without being treated as a HYROX simulation. Half-marathon evidence can strongly support controlled running economy/aerobic durability while contributing little direct station evidence.

## Invariants

- Unmeasured means unknown, not weak.
- Measurement priority is not session prescription.
- Strategic primary-objective evidence outranks convenience or proximity alone.
- Event results only update the capabilities they genuinely test.
- A future primary-objective change dynamically resolves a different hierarchy when one exists.
