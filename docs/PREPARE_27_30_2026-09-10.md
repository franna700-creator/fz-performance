## 27 — Event demand taxonomy v1

Reusable event-demand dimensions are formalised in `config/event-demand-taxonomy.json` and `lib/event-demand-taxonomy.js`. Qualified event formats map into one shared language while retaining event-specific structure.

## 28 — HYROX measurement hierarchy

`config/measurement-hierarchies.json` and `lib/measurement-hierarchy.js` define the primary HYROX evidence priorities and resolve them from the active primary objective. Missing evidence is UNKNOWN, not a weakness score.

## 29 — Recommendation explainability contract

`schemas/recommendation-explanation.schema.json` and `lib/recommendation-explainability.js` define the audit chain and human-facing explanation standard. The contract is prepared but does not activate Tranche 4.2.

## 30 — Golden athlete scenarios v1

`fixtures/golden-athlete-scenarios.json` establishes ten synthetic regression cases spanning discordance, late binding, sparse evidence, AET comparability, multi-session days, missing-vs-zero, athlete override and stale-source behavior.

## Deployment and persistence boundary

These are software/intelligence architecture additions and therefore belong in a controlled candidate release. They do **not** alter production athlete state, do not apply Migration 005, and do not require routine athlete information to trigger deployments. Once deployed, ordinary event additions/changes and athlete feedback use runtime data operations through the existing ingestion standard.
